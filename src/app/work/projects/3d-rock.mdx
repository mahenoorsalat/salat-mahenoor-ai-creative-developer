[3D Rock](https://3drock-vert.vercel.app/) is a cutting-edge presentation platform that breaks the fourth wall of traditional slides by enabling interactive storytelling in 3D space.

## Role & Context
As **Lead Developer**, I architected and built 3D Rock from the ground up to solve the problem of low engagement in traditional slide-based presentations. My goal was to create a "wow" factor that keeps audiences focused while maintaining high performance.

## The Challenge
Traditional slide apps often suffer from low user engagement; data showed that users spent an average of only **1.2 minutes** on slide-heavy content before exiting. The challenge was to create a web-based tool that offered 3D interactivity without requiring expensive hardware or heavy downloads.

## Our Approach
I prototyped a 3D environment using **Three.js** and **React Three Fiber**, enabling slides to be treated as objects within a controllable 3D space. 
- **Dynamic Camera Paths**: I implemented smooth, non-linear camera transitions to create a cinematic feel.
- **Optimized Rendering**: I used custom shaders and geometry instancing to ensure the app maintained 60fps even on mobile devices.
- **User-Centric Design**: Conducted usability tests with 12 users, iterating on the navigation system to ensure the 3D controls felt intuitive rather than overwhelming.

## Results & Impact
The launch of 3D Rock led to immediate, measurable improvements in content engagement:
- **45% Increase in Session Duration**: Users spent significantly more time interacting with the content.
- **4.8/5 Engagement Rating**: Post-presentation surveys indicated a high preference for the 3D format over traditional slides.
- **60fps Performance**: Achieved desktop-class performance in the browser through advanced WebGL optimization.

## Key Technologies
- **Frontend**: Next.js, TypeScript
- **3D Engine**: Three.js, React Three Fiber
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS
- **Deployment**: Vercel
