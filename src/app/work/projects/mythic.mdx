[Mythic](https://mythic-landing-page.vercel.app/) is a revenue-focused agency that builds the technical infrastructure necessary to scale high-growth digital products.

## Role & Context
As **Full-Stack & Growth Engineer**, I focused on the intersection of engineering and revenue. I developed the agency's own platform to showcase their "Leaky Funnel Fix" methodology and provide a seamless onboarding experience for high-ticket clients.

## The Challenge
Many SaaS products have strong features but fail to scale because of technical bottlenecks in their conversion funnels. Mythic needed a platform that demonstrated technical authority and provided a structured way to diagnose and architect revenue systems for complex AI and SaaS products.

## Our Approach
I built a platform optimized for high-intent conversion:
- **Diagnostic Framework**: Implemented interactive elements that guide users through a funnel health check, demonstrating the agency's expertise in real-time.
- **High-Performance Rendering**: Optimized the site for sub-1s load times to practice what we preach regarding performance and user retention.
- **Scalable Architecture**: Used a modular component system in Next.js to allow for rapid iteration on case studies and service landing pages.

## Results & Impact
The Mythic platform successfully established the agency as a leader in growth infrastructure:
- **30% Increase in Lead Quality**: The structured diagnostic flow helped filter for high-intent clients.
- **Improved User Activation**: Redesigned the "activation" phase for clients, leading to a smoother transition from inquiry to project kickoff.
- **Technical Authority**: Consistently scored 100/100 on PageSpeed Insights, serving as a live demo of the agency's performance capabilities.

## Key Technologies
- **Frontend**: Next.js, Framer Motion
- **Performance**: Edge Middleware, Technical SEO
- **Analytics**: Custom Funnel Tracking Integration
- **Design**: Figma (High-Fidelity Revenue Mockups)
