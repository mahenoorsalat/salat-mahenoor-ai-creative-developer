[Autism Warriors Project](https://autismwarriorsproject.com/) is a mission-driven platform dedicated to funding breakthrough autism research and advocating for a neurodiverse world.

## Role & Context
As **Full-Stack Developer**, I built the digital home for the project. The objective was to create an accessible, trustworthy platform that could effectively communicate complex scientific research to a broad audience of parents, researchers, and corporate partners.

## The Challenge
Advocacy platforms need to balance a sense of urgency with high levels of trust. The challenge was to design a site that felt both welcoming to the neurodiverse community and professional enough to attract significant corporate and scientific partnerships.

## Our Approach
I implemented a community-focused design and technical strategy:
- **Accessibility-First UI**: Prioritized WCAG standards to ensure the site is usable by individuals with varying sensory and cognitive needs.
- **Resource Repository**: Built a scalable system for managing and presenting research news, community resources, and donation histories.
- **Next.js for SEO**: Leveraged Next.js to ensure that advocacy content and research results are easily discoverable by search engines, helping the mission reach a global audience.

## Results & Impact
The Autism Warriors platform has become a vital resource for the community:
- **Increased Engagement**: The clear, accessible design led to a measurable lift in user time-on-site and resource downloads.
- **Facilitated Partnerships**: The professional digital presence helped secure new corporate collaborations and research funding.
- **Global Reach**: Technical SEO improvements ensured that critical autism research and advocacy news reached a worldwide audience.

## Key Technologies
- **Stack**: Next.js, React, Node.js
- **Accessibility**: WCAG 2.1 Compliance
- **SEO**: Structured Data for research articles
- **Deployment**: Vercel for fast global delivery
