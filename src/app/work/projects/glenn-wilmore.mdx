[Glenn Wilmore](https://glennwilmore.co.uk/) is a luxury design studio platform that focuses on "engineering digital prestige" for elite entrepreneurs and boutique brands.

## Role & Context
As **Lead Performance Engineer**, I was tasked with ensuring the studio's digital presence matched its premium brand architecture. The focus was on extreme performance optimization and a mobile-first user experience that feels "expensive" and effortless.

## The Challenge
Luxury brands often use high-resolution imagery and complex layouts that can lead to slow load times, especially on mobile devices. The challenge was to maintain a visually stunning, high-fidelity design while achieving industry-leading performance benchmarks (sub-2s load times).

## Our Approach
I applied an "Elite Engineering" strategy:
- **Mobile-First Architecture**: Developed a responsive system that prioritizes mobile performance, ensuring the brand's prestige is maintained across all screen sizes.
- **Advanced Asset Optimization**: Implemented a custom image processing pipeline that delivers high-quality visuals with minimal payload sizes.
- **Micro-Interactions**: Used subtle, refined animations to create a "sensory" user experience that commands attention without being distracting.

## Results & Impact
The Glenn Wilmore ecosystem successfully established a new standard for luxury digital prestige:
- **Sub-2s Page Loads**: Achieved lightning-fast performance across global regions, significantly improving user retention for elite clients.
- **100/100 Core Web Vitals**: Secured a perfect technical score, ensuring the platform is favored by search engines and users alike.
- **Elevated Brand Perception**: Client feedback consistently highlights the "effortless" and "premium" feel of the platform, directly supporting the studio's high-ticket positioning.

## Key Technologies
- **Frontend**: Next.js, Framer Motion
- **Performance**: Image optimization, Technical SEO
- **Styling**: Vanilla CSS (Custom tokens)
- **Design Strategy**: High-Fidelity Brand Architecture
