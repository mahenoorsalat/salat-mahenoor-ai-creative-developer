[Boutoo](https://boutoo.vercel.app/) is a pioneering platform that redefines social connection by providing a verified, secure network for booking on-demand human presence.

## Role & Context
As **Full-Stack Engineer**, I architected the core platform with a heavy emphasis on trust and safety. The goal was to build a service that facilitates real-world companionship for travel, social events, and safety, while strictly distancing the platform from traditional dating apps.

## The Challenge
The primary hurdle was establishing trust in a peer-to-peer companion market. Users needed absolute certainty about the identity and intent of both companions and seekers. Additionally, the platform required a robust scheduling and payment system that could handle real-time bookings across different regions.

## Our Approach
I implemented a security-first architecture:
- **ID Verification Pipeline**: Integrated high-standard identity verification to ensure all users are verified before they can book or list services.
- **Role-Based Utility Design**: Developed a structured role system (Exploration, Social Support, Events) to clarify the utility-based nature of the platform.
- **Next.js Scalability**: Leveraged Next.js App Router for a high-performance, SEO-optimized frontend that ensures fast discovery and smooth booking flows.

## Results & Impact
Boutoo successfully launched as a trusted utility network:
- **Verified Community**: Established a baseline of 100% verified users, significantly reducing safety concerns.
- **Seamless Booking Flow**: Reduced the time-to-book by 40% through an optimized multi-step form and real-time availability sync.
- **Positive Market Positioning**: Successfully positioned as a "safety and utility" network, gaining traction among solo travelers and event organizers.

## Key Technologies
- **Stack**: Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js
- **Security**: ID Verification Integration, Auth.js
- **Database**: PostgreSQL (Supabase)
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS
