[Human Ink](https://human-ink.vercel.app/) is a specialized AI platform designed to transform robotic generative text into natural, emotionally resonant human writing.

## Role & Context
As **Full-Stack Developer**, I built the end-to-end architecture, from the NLP transformation logic to the scalable React frontend. The project was born out of a need for content creators to maintain authenticity in the age of generative AI.

## The Challenge
AI-generated content is increasingly flagged by search engines and detection tools, leading to reduced SEO rankings and loss of reader trust. Content creators needed a way to leverage AI speed while maintaining a truly "human" touch that could pass strict quality benchmarks.

## Our Approach
I developed a multi-layered transformation pipeline:
- **NLP Transformation Layer**: Integrated OpenAI APIs with custom post-processing scripts to break repetitive machine patterns and inject human-like sentence variance.
- **Real-Time Detection API**: Built an integration with AI detection tools to give users a "humanity score" before they export their content.
- **Scalable Backend**: Used Node.js and MongoDB to handle high-volume text processing requests with minimal latency.

## Results & Impact
Human Ink has become a vital tool for digital marketers and writers:
- **90% Detection Evasion**: Content processed through Human Ink consistently bypasses major AI detectors.
- **30% Faster Turnaround**: Users reported significant time savings compared to manual rewriting.
- **1000+ Active Users**: Successfully scaled the platform to support a growing community of creators.

## Key Technologies
- **AI Integration**: OpenAI GPT-4, LangChain
- **Frontend**: Next.js, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express, MongoDB
- **Performance**: Vercel Edge Functions for rapid text processing
