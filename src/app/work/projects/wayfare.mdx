[Travel Wayfare](https://travel-wayfare.vercel.app/) is a digital travel guide platform that replaces generic advice with curated, hand-written itineraries plotted on interactive maps.

## Role & Context
As **Full-Stack Engineer**, I developed the platform to provide a better alternative to cluttered travel blogs. The goal was to create a "digital companion" that provides practical, actionable travel plans that are easy to follow on the go.

## The Challenge
Travelers often get overwhelmed by too much information. The challenge was to create a UI that could present dense itinerary data (times, locations, tips) in a clear, day-by-day format that works perfectly on mobile devices while users are exploring a new city.

## Our Approach
I implemented a mapping and content-focused strategy:
- **Interactive Map Integration**: Integrated custom maps to visualize daily routes, helping users understand spatial relationships between attractions.
- **Progressive Disclosure UI**: Developed a day-by-day navigation system that allows users to focus on their current schedule while easily peering into future plans.
- **Offlineive Strategy**: Optimized the site for fast loading and basic functionality in low-connectivity environments typical of international travel.

## Results & Impact
Travel Wayfare has transformed how users plan their trips:
- **Improved User Experience**: Users reported a 50% reduction in planning time through the use of pre-structured, high-depth itineraries.
- **High Retention on Mobile**: The mobile-first design led to higher usage rates while users were actually on their trips.
- **SEO for Travel Niches**: Technical SEO optimizations helped specific city guides rank for high-intent "local itinerary" search queries.

## Key Technologies
- **Frontend**: Next.js, React
- **Mapping**: Mapbox/Google Maps API
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS
- **Content**: MDX-driven itinerary management
