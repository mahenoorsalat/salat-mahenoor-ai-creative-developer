### Hreflang Implementation & CDN Routing
By implementing `hreflang` tags at the edge, we tell search engines exactly which regional version of a page to show. Whether a client is searching from Berlin (`de-DE`) or Dubai (`en-AE`), our architecture ensures they land on a version of the site that feels intentional and reliable. We use Vercel Edge Middleware to handle geo-routing with sub-50ms latency.
